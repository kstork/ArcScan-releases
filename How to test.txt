ArcScan 0.9.17 - loot marking overlay (test build)

What it does
- Detects an open container, your stash or your inventory in a raid in ARC Raiders on GeForce NOW, without a key press.
- Marks every item slot with a frame colour and a short tag:
  - Cyan ACTIVE n: needed for a quest you set to Active.
  - Green BENCH n: needed for the next level of a workshop station.
  - Green QUEST n: needed for an open quest (or BENCH n: a later workshop level).
  - Green PROJ n: needed for an open phase of a counted project.
  - Green STOCK n: on your "Keep in stock" list.
  - n = how many you still need: everything reserved minus what you own (see "Owned items").
  - What you own covers the reasons in this order: next workshop level, active quests, other quests and
    later workshop levels, projects, keep in stock. The tag names the first reason not yet covered.
  - In your stash, backpack and quick use, a reserved item you own enough of shows "ok" (e.g. QUEST ok) on the
    stacks you need: the biggest stacks are kept whole until the reserve is covered. In containers it is no
    longer marked for the reserve once you own enough.
  - Blue RECYCLE n / SALVAGE n / HOME n: in your stash, backpack and quick use, owned items that ArcScan counts
    as material for a stock item: how many to break down. At base RECYCLE n. In a raid SALVAGE n (break them
    down now to free space) or HOME n (carry them home: recycling there gives more of an important material).
    Your own "Keep in stock" amount of that item is never counted as material.
    This advice only appears once everything for the stock item is there: owned material plus what your owned
    items break down into covers all of it (for a crafted item: every recipe material). Until then those items
    show STOCK n (collect more) or STOCK ok (keep them, do not break them down yet).
  - Pink ALWAYS: on your "Always pick up" list. While a reserve for the item is still open, the reserve marking
    with its number is shown instead; once you own enough, ALWAYS is shown everywhere (also in your stash), until
    your supply drops below the reserve again.
  - Gold VALUE: not needed, but its sell value per kg is at or above your threshold.
  - In your stash, backpack and quick use, the stacks beyond what you need show VALUE n: this stack (n items)
    can be sold. Surplus stacks not worth their weight get the grey frame. The number is only shown once
    ArcScan has seen every stash slot (see "Owned items"); until then VALUE has no number.
  - Grey frame, no tag: not worth taking by these rules.
  - All frames are the same thin line just inside the slot border, so the stack count of the slot stays visible.
  - Orange "?": the item could not be identified with confidence.
- Reads the stack count of every slot (x5, x100) and shows it in the item list in the console window and the log.
  A count it cannot read clearly is shown as "x?". Weapons and gear show no count.
- Markings disappear when the container closes or another window becomes active.
- The overlay is click-through and never takes focus; the game keeps all mouse and keyboard input.

What to check in this version
- Quests that name their items only in the task text are now counted: Shoring Up Defenses (1 ARC Alloy),
  Nothing Ventured (3 Comet Igniter, 3 Rocketeer Driver, 6 ARC Circuitry, 6 ARC Motion Core), A Lay of the Land
  (1 LiDAR Scanner), Taking Stock (3 Industrial Battery). Check them on the settings page (F9, quests).
- The Outpost project "Sheltered Retreat (Outpost)" is in the project list: phase 2 needs 3 Planks, 5 Sheet
  Metal, 10 ARC Alloy, 3 Advanced Electrical Components (from game guides; compare with the game). Phases 1 and 3
  need no items. Set the phases you have done on the settings page, or switch the project off.
- 0.9.16: the stash note went away after one scroll in the All tab in your last log.
- If something is wrong, press F8 while it is on screen and send the files from the "captures" folder together
  with the log file.

Owned items
- ArcScan adds up how many of each item you own: stash plus backpack, quick use and safe pocket.
- The item list in the console window shows the total as "own n" after the count.
- The stash must be sorted by NAME (the sort button above the stash list). Other sort orders give
  wrong totals or none; ArcScan ignores a stash view whose items are visibly out of name order.
- Totals are collected while you look at the stash: an item is counted once all of its stacks are
  visible at the same time. Scroll through the whole stash once so every item is seen.
- Items you no longer have are removed when you look at that part of the stash in the first
  category tab (all items). Other category tabs only add and update items.
- Totals are saved in owned.json next to ArcScan.exe, so they are known during a raid, where the
  stash cannot be seen. In a raid, backpack and quick use are taken from the container screen.
- Equipment (augment, shield, weapons in the loadout) is not counted.
- Completeness: ArcScan reads the stash header ("75/112", All tab) and compares it with the stash slots it has
  seen. Until it has seen every slot, an orange note right of the STASH title says how many slots are missing
  and where to look, e.g. "↑ Ammo  ↓ Wires +1" (the nearest item above and below the current view that
  you moved between backpack and stash). The note waits about 1.5 seconds after a change.
  The stash size is read again in every run. Until the header has been read (or when it cannot be read), there
  is no note and VALUE shows no number; the console says "stash size not read yet".

Settings (F9)
- F9 opens the settings page in your web browser. It runs on this computer only. Changes are saved to
  settings.json next to ArcScan.exe and apply in the game immediately. The page remembers the last tab.
- At the top: the stash status and the button "What do the markings mean?" (colour legend).
- Tab "Quests & my items":
  - Quests: every quest starts as Open and its required items are kept. Set the quests you are working on to
    Active (cyan, they come first) and finished ones to Done (their items are released). Filters: Active + open,
    Active, Open, Done; the search also finds quests by the items they need. "Set all shown quests to" applies
    Open or Done to every quest in the current list.
  - Add an item: one search; each result can be added to Keep in stock or Always pick up.
  - Keep in stock: how many of an item you always want, on top of quests, workshop and projects (reserve 50 +
    stock 30 = keep 80). The list is the priority: the top item gets the materials you own first. Drag the
    handle or use the arrows. While you are short of a stock item, items that break down into it are marked
    too (owned ones count first, at their yield); for a craftable one its recipe materials and the items that
    break down into those. Nothing deeper, and nothing more once you have enough.
  - Always pick up: items that are always marked, without an amount.
- Tab "What I need": everything kept, what you own and what you still need (filter: still needed, all, sources
  and materials). Same numbers as the markings; updates every few seconds.
- Tab "Workshop & projects": each station's current level (higher levels are kept; the next level first) and
  the counted projects with their completed phases. Fully upgraded stations and finished or ended projects are
  hidden ("show"). The Research Station's levels come from game guides until the ARC Data Project has them.
- Tab "Rules":
  - Sources for stock items, combinable: no weapons, modifications, augments, shields or quick-use items (on by
    default); only sources that sell for less than the material they give; a minimum yield; only the best N
    sources per material by yield (on, N = 5; applied last).
  - Carry home or salvage: a source goes HOME when recycling gives more of a material you still need (from the
    chosen rarity, default Common = all) or of any material of a second chosen rarity and above (default Rare)
    than salvaging; otherwise SALVAGE. A sentence on the page says what the two settings do together. Each
    source is counted with the yield of the way it will be broken down.
  - Value threshold: value per kg, default 2000.

Data sources
- Items, icons and quests: MetaForge (metaforge.app/arc-raiders).
- Projects and workshop levels: ARC Data Project, MIT licence (github.com/RaidTheory/arcraiders-data), the data behind arctracker.io.
- Recipes and recycling/salvage yields: MetaForge and the ARC Data Project; where they differ, both are used
  (every material either lists; recipe amounts at the higher, yields at the lower value).
- Items added with Frozen Trail (October 2026) have no recipes in either source yet, so they cannot be planned
  for crafting; this fills in when the sources are updated.
- All of it is cached in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ArcScan and refreshed once per day. A project that is not in the ARC Data Project yet does not appear; add its items under Keep in stock.

Updates
- At every start ArcScan checks github.com/kstork/ArcScan-releases for a newer version (file latest.json).
- If there is one, it downloads it, checks its SHA-256 checksum, replaces ArcScan.exe and this guide, and restarts itself in a new window. settings.json, logs and captures stay as they are.
- If the check or download fails, the current version keeps running.
- Updates are skipped when screenshots are dragged onto the exe.
- Keep ArcScan.exe in a folder you can write to (for example a folder in Documents), not in Program Files.

Requirements
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- Game at 1920x1080. Any monitor works: the monitor of the active window is used.
- Internet on first start (about 30 seconds of downloads).

Windows SmartScreen
- The exe is not signed. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Keys
- F8: read the current screen manually and save a screenshot plus an annotated copy in the "captures" folder.
- F9: open the settings page.
- Only one ArcScan can run at a time; a second start says so and closes.
- While ArcScan runs, the game does not receive F8 and F9.

Log file
- Everything shown in the console window is also written to the "logs" folder next to ArcScan.exe (one file per day), plus every change of the markings and its reason (lines with DEBUG).

Known limits
- 1920x1080 only.
- Automatic detection: containers and the stash at any scroll position. Slots cut off at the top or bottom edge of the stash list are not marked.
- The slot under the mouse cursor is not read; a new slot there is read once the cursor moves away.
- A slot that stays unsure ("?" or "x?") is read again up to 3 times while the screen is still, but only when
  the slot looks different from when it was read (for example after a popup went away).
- Scrolling the stash still lifts all markings until the list stands still again.
- While items are being moved, a half-finished frame can be read for a moment; the next look corrects it.
- When a container reveals its items one by one, a slot can show a wrong item or "?" for a moment until it is
  fully revealed.
- An item that stays "?" in the stash is not counted, so the stash note cannot reach "complete" while it is there.
