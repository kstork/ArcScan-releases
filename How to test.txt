ArcScan 0.8.4 - loot marking overlay (test build)

What it does
- Detects an open container or your stash in ARC Raiders on GeForce NOW, without a key press.
- Marks every item slot with a frame colour and a short tag:
  - Cyan ACTIVE n: needed for a quest you set to Active.
  - Green BENCH n: needed for the next level of a workshop station.
  - Green QUEST n: needed for an open quest (or BENCH n: a later workshop level).
  - Green PROJ n: needed for an open phase of a counted project.
  - Green NEED n: on your list of extra items.
  - n = how many you still need: everything reserved minus what you own (see "Owned items").
  - What you own covers the reasons in this order: active quests, next workshop level, other quests and
    later workshop levels, projects, extra items. The tag names the first reason not yet covered.
  - In your stash, backpack and quick use, a reserved item you own enough of shows "ok" (e.g. QUEST ok):
    keep it. In containers it is no longer marked for the reserve once you own enough.
  - Pink ALWAYS: on your "Always pick up" list. While a reserve for the item is still open, the reserve marking
    with its number is shown instead; once you own enough, ALWAYS is shown everywhere (also in your stash), until
    your supply drops below the reserve again.
  - Gold VALUE: not needed, but its sell value per kg is at or above your threshold.
  - Grey frame, no tag: not worth taking by these rules.
  - All frames are the same thin line just inside the slot border, so the stack count of the slot stays visible.
  - Orange "?": the item could not be identified with confidence.
- Reads the stack count of every slot (x5, x100) and shows it in the item list in the console window and the log.
  A count it cannot read clearly is shown as "x?". Weapons and gear show no count.
- Markings disappear when the container closes or another window becomes active.
- The overlay is click-through and never takes focus; the game keeps all mouse and keyboard input.

What to check in this version
- An "Always pick up" item you own enough of shows pink ALWAYS, also in the stash (instead of e.g. QUEST ok).
- Rosary (and other items that match their icon clearly but not perfectly) should no longer show "?".
- Less flicker: slots with faint borders (blurry stream frames) are found again, blurry counts are read, a stack
  count change no longer lifts the marking, and an item that stays "?" is only read again when the slot changed.
- Scroll through the stash: owned totals ("own n" in the console, F9 Reserve list) should no longer drop to 0
  and come back.
- Markings now show how many you still need. Open the settings page (F9): the Reserve list shows own, keep and
  still need per item. Compare a few with the markings in a container and in the stash.
- Add an item under "Always pick up" and check it gets a pink ALWAYS marking (when no reserve is open for it).
- Sort the stash by name, then scroll through it once from top to bottom. Compare a few "own n" values in the
  console with what you have (stash plus backpack and quick use). Split, move or sell a stack and check again.
- Switch between stash tabs: a "?" caused by the category name popping up over a slot should disappear about
  a second after the popup.
- If something is wrong, press F8 while it is on screen and send the files from the "captures" folder together
  with the log file.

Owned items
- ArcScan adds up how many of each item you own: stash plus backpack, quick use and safe pocket.
- The item list in the console window shows the total as "own n" after the count.
- The stash must be sorted by NAME (the sort button above the stash list). Other sort orders give
  wrong totals or none; ArcScan ignores a stash view whose items are visibly out of name order.
- Totals are collected while you look at the stash: an item is counted once all of its stacks are
  visible at the same time. Scroll through the whole stash once so every item is seen.
- Items you no longer have are removed when you look at that part of the stash in the first
  category tab (all items). Other category tabs only add and update items.
- Totals are saved in owned.json next to ArcScan.exe, so they are known during a raid, where the
  stash cannot be seen. In a raid, backpack and quick use are taken from the container screen.
- Equipment (augment, shield, weapons in the loadout) is not counted.

Settings (F9)
- F9 opens the settings page in your web browser. It runs on this computer only.
- Quests: every quest starts as Open and its required items are kept in reserve. Set finished quests to Done to release their items. Set the quests you are working on to Active to highlight their items in cyan. "Set all shown quests to" applies Open or Done to every quest in the current list (use the search box to narrow it, e.g. by trader).
- Projects: ticked projects keep all their open phases in reserve. Choose how many phases you have completed. Projects past their end date start unticked.
- Workshop: choose each station's current level. All higher levels are kept in reserve.
- Reserve: the combined amount per item and where it comes from.
- Extra items you need: amounts here are added on top of the reserve (reserve 50 + extra 30 = keep 80).
- Always pick up: items that are always marked, without an amount.
- Value threshold: value per kg, default 2000.
- Changes are saved to settings.json next to ArcScan.exe and apply in the game immediately.

Data sources
- Items, icons and quests: MetaForge (metaforge.app/arc-raiders).
- Projects and workshop levels: ARC Data Project, MIT licence (github.com/RaidTheory/arcraiders-data), the data behind arctracker.io.
- Both are cached in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ArcScan and refreshed once per day. A project that is not in the ARC Data Project yet does not appear; add its items under Extra items.

Updates
- At every start ArcScan checks github.com/kstork/ArcScan-releases for a newer version (file latest.json).
- If there is one, it downloads it, checks its SHA-256 checksum, replaces ArcScan.exe and this guide, and restarts itself in a new window. settings.json, logs and captures stay as they are.
- If the check or download fails, the current version keeps running.
- Updates are skipped when screenshots are dragged onto the exe.
- Keep ArcScan.exe in a folder you can write to (for example a folder in Documents), not in Program Files.

Requirements
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- Game at 1920x1080. Any monitor works: the monitor of the active window is used.
- Internet on first start (about 30 seconds of downloads).

Windows SmartScreen
- The exe is not signed. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Keys
- F8: read the current screen manually and save a screenshot plus an annotated copy in the "captures" folder.
- F9: open the settings page.
- While ArcScan runs, the game does not receive F8 and F9.

Log file
- Everything shown in the console window is also written to the "logs" folder next to ArcScan.exe (one file per day), plus every change of the markings and its reason (lines with DEBUG).

Known limits
- 1920x1080 only.
- Automatic detection: containers and the stash at any scroll position. Slots cut off at the top or bottom edge of the stash list are not marked.
- The slot under the mouse cursor is not read; a new slot there is read once the cursor moves away.
- A slot that stays unsure ("?" or "x?") is read again up to 3 times while the screen is still, but only when
  the slot looks different from when it was read (for example after a popup went away).
- Scrolling the stash still lifts all markings until the list stands still again.
- Stack counts are read and listed, but not used yet: the marking still shows how many to keep in total and does not yet stop once you own that many.
